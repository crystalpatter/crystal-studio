Crystal Studio: privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Crystal Studio is a private tool used only by Crystal Patter of Crystal Cosmetics (Shop 4, 195 Varsity Parade, Varsity Lakes QLD 4227) to publish posts to her own Instagram account (@bycrystalcosmetics) and Facebook Page, and to view their statistics. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in.

What it uses

  • Photos, videos and captions that Crystal chooses to publish. These are stored with our hosting provider (Vercel) so Instagram and Facebook can collect them.
  • An access token for Crystal Cosmetics' Facebook Page and linked Instagram account, kept as an encrypted setting with the hosting provider.
  • Statistics Meta provides about the account and its posts (for example reach, saves and profile visits). These are aggregated numbers, not information about individual people.
  • Preview images and notes are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to draft captions.

Crystal Studio does not collect information about followers or other Facebook or Instagram users, does not sell data, and does not use it for advertising.

Deleting data

To remove the app's access, remove "Crystal Studio" under Facebook → Settings → Business integrations. To ask for any data to be deleted, email info@crystalcosmetics.com.au and it will be deleted within 30 days.
