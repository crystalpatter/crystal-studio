# Crystal Studio: how to post (2-minute guide)

Open **CC Studio** from your home screen.

## Make a post

1. **Choose photos or a video.**
   - One photo makes a photo post. Several photos (up to 10) make a carousel. One video makes a Reel.
   - Photos are cropped to Instagram's shape for you. In a carousel they all match the first photo.
   - Tap **×** to remove one.
2. **Caption.**
   - Tap the kind of post (Healed result, Education, Behind the scenes, Taking bookings, Brow lamination offer).
   - Optional: add a few words in **Notes for the AI**, e.g. *"healed lip blush 6 weeks, client wanted soft rosy pink"*.
   - Tap **✨ Write my caption**. You get three options. Tap **Use this one**, then edit anything you like.
   - Check the line **"The AI sees: …"**. If it misread the photo, fix your notes and tap ✨ again.
   - ⚠ warnings appear if a caption breaks your brief (e.g. "best", "painless", a client count, hair strokes). Fix them before posting.
3. **Where and when.**
   - Instagram is always on. **Also post to Facebook** is on by default.
   - **Post now**, or **Schedule** and pick a time. The Mon / Wed / Fri 6pm buttons follow your 30-day plan.
4. **Review post** → check the preview → **Post now** / **Schedule**.
   - Videos and carousels take a minute or two while Instagram processes them. You can close the app; it finishes on its own.
   - When it says **Posted ✓**, tap **View on Instagram** to see it.

## Calendar

- 🟤 scheduled · 🟢 posted · 🔴 needs attention. Shaded days are your suggested Mon/Wed/Fri slots with nothing booked yet.
- Tap a day to see its posts. On a scheduled post: **Post now**, **Change time**, or **Cancel**.
- If something failed, the reason is shown in plain English. Tap **Retry**.
- Removing a post from the calendar doesn't delete it from Instagram. Do that in the Instagram app.

## Insights

The last 28 days: followers, accounts reached, views, profile visits, link taps and engagement, plus reach, saves and shares for your recent posts. Instagram can take up to 48 hours to finish counting. Tap **Refresh** for the latest.

**What to watch:** saves and shares on education posts (people keeping your advice), and profile visits plus link taps (people heading to book).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Connection expired"** → Settings → **Reconnect Instagram & Facebook**, then paste the new token into Vercel and redeploy (setup guide, Part 4).
- **The app is down or confusing** → use **Meta Business Suite** to post. It does the same Instagram + Facebook posting and scheduling, for free.
- **Ads:** not in the app yet. Ads always cost money paid to Meta. Use Meta Business Suite → **Ads** to see or pause anything running.
