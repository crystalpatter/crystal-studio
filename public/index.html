      Connect Instagram & Facebook

      You log in on Facebook's own page. This app never sees your password. When you come back it's connected: there is nothing to copy or paste.

      ElevenLabs Optional

      Connect an ElevenLabs account so the Hub can create voiceovers, music and sound for your posts.

      Where to get the key: elevenlabs.io → sign in → Developers → API Keys → Create key → copy it and paste it here. It's checked with ElevenLabs and stored encrypted; it is never shown again. ElevenLabs' free plan doesn't allow business use, so posts for the business need its Starter plan (about US$6 a month).
