Marketing Hub: privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Marketing Hub (registered with Meta as "Crystal Studio") is a private tool used only by Crystal Patter of Crystal Cosmetics (Shop 4, 195 Varsity Parade, Varsity Lakes QLD 4227) to publish posts to her own Instagram account (@bycrystalcosmetics) and Facebook Page, and to view their statistics. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in.

What it uses

  • Photos, videos and captions that Crystal chooses to publish. These are stored with our hosting provider (Vercel) so Instagram and Facebook can collect them.
  • An access token for Crystal Cosmetics' Facebook Page and linked Instagram account, kept as an encrypted setting with the hosting provider.
  • Statistics Meta provides about the account and its posts (for example reach, saves and profile visits). These are aggregated numbers, not information about individual people.
  • Preview images and notes are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to draft captions.

To help Crystal reply to people who engage with her posts, the app reads the public comments and Facebook reactions on Crystal Cosmetics' own posts and keeps a list of the commenter's public profile name, their comment and the date for up to 6 months. It does not collect phone numbers, email addresses or private messages, does not sell or share this list, and uses it only so Crystal can reply. Anyone can ask for their entry to be removed by emailing info@crystalcosmetics.com.au, or by deleting their comment.

Deleting data

To remove the app's access, remove "Crystal Studio" under Facebook → Settings → Business integrations. To ask for any data to be deleted, email info@crystalcosmetics.com.au and it will be deleted within 30 days.
